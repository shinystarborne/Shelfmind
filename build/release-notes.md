## New
- **Crash recovery** — if the app window ever fails to start, crashes, or stays blank after an update, ShelfMind now shows a built-in recovery dialog offering to restart or automatically install the previous release over the broken one. Your library data is always kept separately and is never touched.

## Fixes
- **App wouldn't open after updating to beta.4** — a missing import crashed the renderer on launch, leaving a blank window. Sorry about that; it now boots normally.
- **Continue Reading covers** — the banner's cover cards render at their intended small size instead of blowing up to full width.
